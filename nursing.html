What we offer

Daily Living Activities Assistance

You need a combination of professional knowledge and sincere human support when looking for care for a loved one. With a focus on holistic support that preserves dignity and independence, TrueLife Home Healthcare offers professional home nursing care that Dubai residents rely on. Our dedication to daily living assistance is provided through personal care, meal preparation, mobility assistance, and companionship for a healthier, independent life, combining these essential everyday supports with expert home nursing so your loved one can flourish in the comfort and familiarity of their own home.

Acute and Chronic Care

When managing complex or long‑term health conditions, continuous, professional medical care is essential. TrueLife Home Healthcare provides acute and chronic care through dedicated DHA‑licensed home nurses, ensuring medical excellence without the stress of constant clinic visits. With expert medical support, symptom management and personalized treatment for improved health and recovery, our acute home nursing care in Dubai brings clinical expertise into a familiar setting, supporting better compliance, lower anxiety and a reduced risk of hospital readmission.

Elderly Care

As our loved ones age, maintaining their comfort, health and dignity becomes the highest priority. TrueLife Home Healthcare offers specialized elderly home nursing care — a comprehensive solution delivered by compassionate, DHA‑licensed home nurses right to your doorstep. With personalized support, health management and assistance with daily activities to enhance quality of life, our home nursing service in Dubai removes the upheaval of moving to a facility and allows elders to remain rooted in their familiar environment, which is shown to support emotional well‑being and cognitive health.

Dementia Care

When managing cognitive challenges like dementia or Alzheimer’s, stability and familiarity are vital for maintaining comfort and reducing anxiety. TrueLife Home Healthcare provides expert dementia home nursing — specialized care delivered by compassionate, DHA‑licensed home nurses in the one place that holds comfort and memory: the patient’s home. With compassionate support, safety monitoring and personalized activities to enhance comfort and well‑being, our dementia care at home service helps prevent the distress and accelerated decline often associated with moving to unfamiliar facilities.

Wound Care

Wound healing requires consistency, sterility and expert monitoring. TrueLife Home Healthcare provides specialized wound care home nursing — delivering advanced treatment, infection prevention and personalized healing plans for faster recovery right in the comfort and safety of your own environment. By choosing our home nursing for wound dressing services, you avoid painful, stressful trips to clinics while benefiting from consistent care in a controlled home setting that significantly reduces exposure to pathogens and supports the body’s natural healing process.

Post‑Surgical Care

Post-surgical recovery demands close supervision, pain control, and precise clinical support to prevent complications and ensure proper healing. TrueLife Home Healthcare provides professional post-surgical home nursing care, delivering continuous monitoring, medication management, wound assessment, and mobility support—so patients can recover safely and comfortably at home.With TrueLife, care comes to you—professional, convenient, and focused on your safety and well-being. Our experienced, DHA-licensed nurses help ease common post-operative challenges such as pain, swelling, limited mobility, and infection risk through personalized care plans tailored to each procedure and recovery stage. By receiving post-surgical nursing at home, patients avoid exhausting hospital visits, reduce the risk of hospital-acquired infections, and benefit from focused, one-on-one care in a familiar environment that promotes faster, more confident recovery.

Blood Collection | IV Therapy

Blood tests collections are essential for accurate diagnosis, treatment, and recovery—but frequent trips to clinics or hospitals can be stressful, time-consuming, and uncomfortable. TrueLife Home Healthcare brings professional blood collection and directly to your home, ensuring a safe, efficient, and patient-focused experience.Our DHA-licensed nurses perform precise blood draws and samples while maintaining strict sterility standards and adhering to clinical best practices. Patients benefit from minimized discomfort, real-time monitoring, and personalized care in the comfort and privacy of their own home. Whether it’s routine blood work, medication infusions, or post-operative IV treatments our home nursing services eliminate unnecessary hospital visits, reduce exposure to pathogens, and provide peace of mind for both patients and their families.

Enteral Feeding

Maintaining proper nutrition is critical for patients who are unable to eat orally due to illness, surgery, or chronic conditions. TrueLife Home Healthcare provides specialized enteral feeding services at home—ensuring safe, precise, and consistent nutritional support tailored to each patient’s needs.Our DHA-licensed nurses are trained in managing nasogastric (NG) tubes, gastrostomy (G-tube) feeding, and other enteral feeding methods. We monitor feeding schedules, manage tube care, prevent complications, and provide education to patients and caregivers to ensure optimal safety and comfort.Receiving enteral feeding at home means patients avoid stressful hospital visits while benefiting from continuous professional supervision, infection prevention, and personalized care in a familiar environment. This not only supports proper nutrition and recovery but also improves overall quality of life for patients and peace of mind for their families.

Tracheostomy Care

Managing a tracheostomy requires precision, hygiene, and continuous monitoring to prevent complications such as infection, airway blockage, or respiratory distress. TrueLife Home Healthcare provides professional tracheostomy care at home, ensuring expert guidance, safety, and comfort for patients and their families.Our DHA-licensed nurses are trained to perform routine suctioning, tube cleaning, stoma care, and airway monitoring, while educating caregivers on proper techniques, emergency precautions, and maintenance protocols. We deliver personalized care plans that are tailored to each patient’s condition and recovery needs.By choosing home tracheostomy care, patients avoid stressful hospital visits and receive consistent, specialized support in a safe, familiar environment. This not only reduces the risk of infection and complications but also promotes better respiratory health, comfort, and peace of mind for both patients and caregivers.

Catheter Care

Catheter management requires strict hygiene, monitoring, and professional expertise to prevent infections, blockages, and discomfort. TrueLife Home Healthcare offers specialized home catheter care, providing safe, consistent, and compassionate support tailored to each patient’s needs.Our DHA-licensed nurses perform routine catheter care, including insertion assistance, maintenance, cleaning, monitoring for complications, and patient education on proper hygiene and self-care. Each care plan is personalized, ensuring comfort, dignity, and safety while reducing the risk of urinary tract infections and other complications.For home catheter care, patients avoid frequent hospital visits, receive one-on-one professional supervision, and recover in a safe, familiar environment that promotes comfort and well-being. Families gain peace of mind knowing that their loved ones are receiving expert care every step of the way.
