How we help

🧠

Neurological Rehabilitation

We help patients with stroke, paralysis, Parkinson’s disease, dementia-related mobility challenges, and multiple sclerosis. We focus on improving balance, coordination, and movement to help reduce falls and boost confidence at home.

🦴

Pain Management

Truelife provides relief for back and neck pain, joint pain in the knees, shoulders, and hips, and conditions like arthritis or osteoarthritis. We also help manage sciatica and nerve pain, and improve posture caused by long hours of sitting or daily strain, so you can move more comfortably every day.

🧓

Geriatric Physiotherapy

We provide tailored physiotherapy programs to the needs of elderly, in the convenience and security of their own homes. Gentle exercises are designed for seniors to improve balance, strength, and flexibility, reduce pain, and prevent falls, helping them stay safe, active, and independent at home. Our licensed physiotherapist uses individualised therapy to regain their mobility and quality of life.

🩺

Post Surgery Recovery

Through personalized therapy, pain management, and mobility support, we help patients regain strength, function, and independence after surgery. It is essential to begin physiotherapy at home following surgery for conditions such as knee replacement, hip replacement, or ligament repair. This lowers the possibility of complications following surgery, guiding you through safe exercises to regain strength, reduce pain, and move comfortably again.

🧒

Pediatric Physiotherapy

Our DHA-licensed team of physiotherapists tailor kid-friendly care plans to support children to improve their physical, cognitive, and developmental abilities. We provide exercises to build strength, improve coordination, and help your child move, play, and manage daily activities more easily.

🏃

Sports Injury Management

Programs to help prevent injuries, build strength and flexibility, and recover from muscle strains or ligament tears. We also provide post-surgery rehabilitation tailored to your sport or activity, helping you return safely and confidently.

🤱

Post/Pre‑natal Physiotherapy

Care for back and pelvic pain during pregnancy, support for recovery after childbirth, and gentle exercises to rebuild core strength. We also help with pelvic floor issues like bladder control and recovery after a C section, including scar care.
